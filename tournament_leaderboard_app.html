User Guide

The Event Filters

The dropdown filters in the left sidebar let you narrow down the leaderboard by specific event details (like Host, Format, Duration, Points, or Event Name). If you leave all the filters blank, the table automatically defaults to showing the full list of every player across all recorded matches. When you adjust the filters, the ELO, MoV Elo, number of games, and win rate will automatically adjust to reflect only the data for the filters you have applied. Changing any filter will instantly update the standings on the fly.

Standard ELO: The Basic Skill Score

Standard ELO is your basic skill score. Everyone starts at 1500 points. When you win a match, you take points from the other player, and when you lose, you give points to them.

The system also looks at who you are playing. If you beat a very good player, you get a lot of points because it is a big upset. If you beat a brand new player, you only get a few points because you were supposed to win. In the same way, losing to a beginner hurts your score a lot, but losing to a pro does not hurt nearly as much.

MoV ELO: The Score-Gap Score

MoV ELO works the exact same way, but it also looks at your final score to see how hard you won or lost.

If you win a game by just a tiny bit, your score goes up a little. If you crush your opponent and win by a huge margin, your score goes up a lot. This helps when you lose, too. If you fight hard and lose by just one point, your penalty is small. But if you get completely wiped out, your score drops much more.
